Privacy

No trackers.
No cookies.

This site collects nothing about you while you browse. Here is the whole policy, in plain English.

The short version

No analytics, no trackers, no cookies, no advertising networks. There is nothing to opt out of because there is nothing being collected.

The contact form

If you fill in the form, your name, phone, email and message are sent to the shop’s own mailbox so somebody can answer you. Nothing is stored here on the site, nothing is sold, and nothing is added to any list.

The map

Directions and the embedded map are served by Google. When a page with the map loads, Google receives a standard request for the map tiles, subject to Google’s own privacy terms.
